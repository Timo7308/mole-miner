# ⛏️ Mole Miner

**Mole Miner** is a 2D arcade-style game developed with **Processing**.

The player controls a small mole digging through underground levels, collecting diamonds and gold while avoiding hostile creatures such as bats and skeletons.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

The objective is to collect as many valuable resources as possible while progressing through two underground levels.

### Level 1

* Dig through the underground environment
* Collect diamonds and gold
* Avoid bats and skeletons
* Reach the blue portal to continue

### Level 2

* Continue collecting diamonds and gold
* Avoid enemies
* Reach the treasure chest to complete the game

---

## ✨ Features

* Self-designed pixel art
